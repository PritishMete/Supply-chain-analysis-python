## 📊 Overview

As someone transitioning into data analytics, I wanted to work on a real-world business problem that reflects the type of challenges analysts face in supply chain and retail operations.

This project analyzes supply chain data to understand how factors such as **delivery performance, shipping methods, customer segments, product categories, sales, profitability, and order behavior** affect overall business performance.

The analysis was performed using **Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**, with the results documented through exploratory analysis and visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Supply chain operations involve multiple interconnected factors, including:

- Order fulfillment
- Shipping performance
- Delivery delays
- Customer segments
- Product categories
- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Geographic distribution
- Shipping methods

The objective of this project is to use data analysis to identify patterns and potential operational issues that can help businesses improve:

- Delivery performance
- Supply chain efficiency
- Customer experience
- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Operational decision-making

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are to:

1. Analyze overall sales and order performance.
2. Evaluate delivery and shipping performance.
3. Identify patterns in late deliveries.
4. Compare different shipping modes.
5. Analyze sales and profitability across product categories.
6. Evaluate customer segment performance.
7. Analyze geographic sales distribution.
8. Identify factors associated with supply chain performance.
9. Perform exploratory data analysis to uncover meaningful business patterns.
10. Translate analytical findings into actionable business insights.
